The game

IMUNO is a single-player idle game about the immune system. It works offline; an internet connection is used only to show the ads you choose to watch, to verify purchases and ad rewards, and, on Android, to send anonymous statistics as described in the privacy policy: https://marcosrg3003.github.io/imuno/privacy/
